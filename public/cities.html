Sustainable cities and communities

The first sustainable goal we have chosen is to create new sustainable cities/communities and to improve the sustainability of existing cities/communities.

Find out more

Why this goal matters

Sustainable cities and Communities means future and current cities and human settlements will be more inclusive, safe, resilient and sustainable than ever before

Possible policies to make change at the local level

Target 11.1 of the sustainable cities and communities goal states that by 2030 we should have ensured that all have access to adequate, safe and affordable housing and basic services. As policy makers you have the power to push through legislation in your local area that combats homelessness and inadequate housing.

Target 11.4 of the sustainable cities and communities goal is to strengthen efforts to protect and safegaurd the world's cultural and natural heritage. As a policy maker you have the power to ensure nobody's culture or heritage is threatened, you can do this by encouraging education in schools on the topic and holding fundraising events to fund the protection of world heritage sites.

            

Target 11.6 of the sustainable cities and communities goal is to by 2030, reduce the adverse per capita environmental impact of cities, including by paying specicial attention to air quality and municipal and other waste management, as a policy maker you can encourage legislation and education on recycling and its importance to ensure responsible waste management.
