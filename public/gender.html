Possible policies to make change at the local level

Target 5.5 of the sustainable development goals is to Ensure women's full and effective participation in leadership and decision-making. As policy makers you should ensure inclusion of women and hire more women in prominent roles

Target 5.3 of the sustainable development goals is to eliminate forced marriages and any other harmful practices such as female genital mutilation. While this isn't an issue local to your area it is still important that you take part in action to help get rid of these practices world wide as they are still prevalent issues in many parts of the world. As a policy maker you could help push for initiatives to raise awareness and help facilitate charity functions in your area. You could also push for legislation that criminalises taking women children abroad to countries where it is legal to force them to marry

            

Target 5.2 of the SDG is to end all violence against women and girls. As policy makers you have the power to protect women from violence and exploitation. You can create policies to enforce women's safety and prevent exploitation like policing dangerous areas and providing better avenues for reporting sexual crimes. This goal is especially important as in the past couple decades sexual violence against women and girls has risen significantly showing a lack of action to protect women and girls
