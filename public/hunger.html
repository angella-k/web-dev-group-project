Why this goal matters

Zero hunger means less children than ever before will be born into food insecurity ensuring equal chances for all from the get go

Possible policies to make change at the local level

Target 2.1 states that by 2030 the aim is to end hunger and ensure access by all people, in particular the poor and people in vulnerable situations, including infants, to safe, nutritious and sufficient food all year round. As a policy maker you play a significant role in determining funding for food banks, a heavily relied on resource, by allocating more funding you can make sure less children go hungry

Target 2.a focuses on increasing investment especially in rural infrastructure, agricultural research etc. as a policy maker you can ensure farmers are protected and supported.

Target 2.c focuses on adopting measures to ensure the proper functioning of food commodity markets and their derivatives and facilitate timely access to market information, including on food reserves, in order to help limit extreme food price volatility, as a policymaker you can ensure there is adequate food reserves so that everyone in your community can access affordable food.
